# Smart Vision AI PPT Content

## Slide 1: Title
Smart Vision AI: AI-powered Android assistant for OCR, translation, voice, medicine and waste classification.

## Slide 2: Problem Statement
People need quick mobile help to read text, translate information, identify useful visual categories and interact through voice.

## Slide 3: Objectives
Build a modern Android app using Kotlin, XML, CameraX, ML Kit, TensorFlow Lite, Room, DataStore and Firebase-ready authentication.

## Slide 4: System Architecture
Presentation layer: Fragments and ViewModels.
Data layer: Repositories, Room and DataStore.
AI layer: ML Kit OCR/Translation, TFLite task-vision and voice APIs.

## Slide 5: Modules
OCR Scanner, Translator, Voice Assistant, Medicine Scanner, Waste Classifier, AI Chat, History, Settings and Profile.

## Slide 6: UI Design
Dark blue futuristic theme, glass cards, neon gradients, particle animation, rounded controls and Material 3 components.

## Slide 7: Testing
Unit tests for repository/model logic, Android UI smoke tests, permission text test and build validation through Gradle.

## Slide 8: Results
The app builds successfully as a debug APK and demonstrates all major college mini-project modules.

## Slide 9: Future Scope
Custom trained models, cloud AI answers, PDF export, accessibility reading mode and multilingual offline packs.
