# Smart Vision AI Demo Script

1. Launch the app and show the animated splash screen.
2. Swipe onboarding pages and explain the AI modules.
3. Login using email/password demo mode or Google sign-in after Firebase setup.
4. Open dashboard and describe the feature cards and analytics panel.
5. Open OCR Scanner, grant camera permission and scan printed text.
6. Copy the OCR output, then open Translator and translate sample text.
7. Use Voice Assistant and say: "Open OCR" or "Open waste classifier".
8. Open Medicine Scanner and press Analyze Medicine to show confidence and safety warning.
9. Open Waste Classifier and press Classify Waste to show recycling suggestion.
10. Open AI Chat and ask: "Explain OCR".
11. Open History to show stored scans and chat results.
12. Open Profile and Settings, then explain Firebase-ready Google connection.
